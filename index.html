Quickest way: You can delete your account directly inside the app, without using this page.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Real Estate Mogul app and log in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the More tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion. Your account will be permanently deleted within 3 weeks, and you will not be able to log in again after confirming.

How to request deletion from this page

If you cannot delete your account in the app, send a deletion request by email to emekaekeocha1@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, and include:

  • The subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  • The email address of the account you want deleted
  • Your full name (as shown on your profile)

We will process your request and confirm within 7 days. To protect your account, we may ask you to verify ownership before processing the deletion.

What data is deleted or kept

Deleted: your profile, property listings, messages, connections, hire requests, and associated files.

Kept for a limited time: your email address and a record of the deletion may be retained in backups for up to 12 months after deletion for legal purposes. This data is not used for any other purpose.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

If you only want to remove specific content, you can delete individual property listings and media from inside the app at any time, without deleting your account.
